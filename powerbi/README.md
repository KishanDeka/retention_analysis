# Power BI dashboard

Use `data/processed/powerbi_customer_retention.csv` generated by `scripts_export_powerbi.py`
or build the same table in Power Query.

Recommended pages:

## 1. Executive Overview
Cards:
- Customers
- Churn rate
- Monthly revenue
- Revenue at risk
- High-risk customers
- Expected campaign profit

Charts:
- Churn by contract
- Churn by tenure group
- Revenue at risk by segment

## 2. Churn Drivers
- Churn by monthly charge band
- Churn by payment method
- Churn by tech support
- Churn by risk segment
- slicers for contract, service, tenure, payment method

## 3. Retention Experiment
- Control churn rate
- Treatment churn rate
- Absolute churn reduction
- Posterior probability treatment is better
- Expected campaign profit / ROI
- Targeted customer count
